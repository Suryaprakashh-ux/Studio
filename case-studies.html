01 · 12-month engagement

Manifestation Bootcamp

Client

A Delhi-based mentor and healer

12-month partnership

A trust-led online manifestation bootcamp had a strong offer, but growth depended on limited reach and organic enquiries. The work brought the ad, landing page, and WhatsApp conversation together into one booking journey.

~10 / week Registrations at the start
3× Sales within the first month
25–30 / day Bookings after scaling

The initial figure is weekly registrations; the later figure is daily bookings, as reported in the source.

The challenge

Build a repeatable way to reach new audiences and convert interest into confirmed bookings, without losing the personal, credible feel the offer depends on.

The approach

Own the hand-offs from Facebook and Instagram ads to a focused mobile landing page, then promptly respond to and follow up with prospects on WhatsApp.

Meta Ads Landing page WhatsApp Booking

What I managed

  • Audience & campaignsTargeting around personal growth, mindfulness, and manifestation; testing audiences and messages.
  • Creative & pageEmotion-led creative with consistent messaging and a mobile-friendly page with one clear call to action.
  • Enquiry follow-upPrompt WhatsApp replies, answers to bootcamp questions, and follow-up with leads who had not confirmed.
  • Budget optimisationShifted spend toward stronger campaigns and creatives and paused weaker ads early.
↗

Why it worked The promise stayed consistent from ad to page to chat, and faster follow-up met prospects while their interest was high.

02 · 9-month engagement

Sleep Masterclass

Client

A lifestyle coach with a sleep masterclass

9-month partnership

In a crowded wellness space, generic messages can be easy to scroll past. Campaigns used specific sleep-related pain points and added YouTube to Meta, while keeping the landing page and WhatsApp follow-up consistent.

2–3 / day Bookings at the start
3× Sales within the first month
13–14 / day Bookings after scaling

The challenge

Grow daily bookings in a competitive wellness category, while reaching more people without losing lead quality or making the booking journey feel difficult.

The approach

Pair Meta feed campaigns with YouTube video ads to broaden reach; direct both channels to one focused page and a timely WhatsApp conversation.

Meta + YouTube Landing page WhatsApp Booking

What I managed

  • Multi-channel campaignsMeta audience and creative testing, plus YouTube video ads to reach people beyond the social feed.
  • Pain-point creativeMultiple hooks about tiredness, stress, and low energy rather than generic wellness claims.
  • Landing pageA mobile-friendly page that introduced the masterclass and made the next step clear.
  • Follow-up & budgetPrompt WhatsApp enquiry handling and follow-up; spend moved toward stronger campaigns and creatives.
↗

Why it worked YouTube widened reach, specific creative spoke to a real pain point, and every channel fed the same conversion path.
